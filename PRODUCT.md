## Users

The current company website is oriented toward prospective partners, suppliers, investors, and collaborators. End users are outside the website's current target audience. This does not define the eventual users or buyers of the autonomous-access project; those roles remain open.

## Product Purpose

Rh45 is an early-stage company. Its About page states its purpose as redefining how the physical world supports human life, its mission as enabling the places where people live, work and gather to become autonomous, adaptive and secure, on foundations designed to evolve, and its vision as a world where the places people inhabit anticipate needs, protect life and adapt over time while remaining accountable to the people they serve. The company is beginning work on its first concrete project, focused on autonomous access.

## Positioning

The foundational text is the Rh45 Foundational Direction (Revised) doc. It says Rh45 exists to give the physical world the ability to think. Whether Rh45 is a horizontal platform or a vertically integrated product company is a Strategic Architecture decision, so public copy does not use "cognitive layer" or "we do not build isolated products" as identity claims. It describes intelligence as distributed across edge, cloud and human context, with open interfaces at system boundaries. The exact architecture and mechanism behind this positioning remain unconfirmed. Preserve the conceptual strength of the idea while being precise about which systems sense, reason, coordinate, or act.

The site currently addresses prospective partners, suppliers, investors, and collaborators rather than end users. The company's first concrete project is focused on autonomous access; its setting, workflow, user roles, and scope have not been confirmed. The About page sets out purpose, mission, vision, principles, values, beliefs, and non-negotiables for the company.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed company stage: early-stage, beginning work on a concrete autonomous-access project.
- The autonomous-access project's requirements, architecture, current implementation status, and available capabilities remain unconfirmed.
- The About page describes autonomous environments as company purpose, mission, and vision; it does not define a technical implementation.
- The website says Rh45 "exists to" give the physical world the ability to think, and calls the sense, reason, coordinate and act cycle "the infrastructure Rh45 is building." Do not infer shipped capabilities from those phrases.
- Keep strategic beliefs and future capabilities distinct from verified implementation.
- The website is a static Astro project; its pages are in `src/pages/` and its deployment instructions are in `README.md`.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the company name Rh45.
- The company is early-stage; describe its direction and current operating reality accurately.
- Retain conceptual force while maintaining technical precision. The user rejected copy that became generic or technically inaccurate.
- Preserve the About page's stated commitments: purpose before technology; distributed intelligence and open boundaries; autonomy with accountability; foundations that can evolve; and human-centered systems.
- Treat human safety, security and privacy, reliability and rigor, transparency, and no artificial lock-in as non-negotiables stated by About.
- Do not invent customers, deployed products, results, or technical architecture.

## Product Principles

- Put real human needs before technology, with safety and security as foundational constraints.
- Design for distributed intelligence and interoperability across systems, infrastructure, and human context.
- Pair autonomy with accountability, transparency, reliability, and meaningful human oversight.
- Build durable foundations for long-term adaptation rather than disconnected products or short-term gains.
- Preserve the force of Rh45's direction while distinguishing strategic ambition from verified technical capability.
